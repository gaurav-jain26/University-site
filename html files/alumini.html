ALUMINI

    Reconnect with the Apes Community

    Your connection to Apes does not end after Commencement. Our alumni are a vital source of connection and philanthropy that help Apes achieve its mission to educate the citizens and citizen-leaders for our society through the transformative power of a liberal arts and sciences education.

    Stay Connected

    Attending Apes is only the beginning. After graduation, stay connected with your classmates, and with our faculty, through the Apes Alumni Association. Participate in one of our Global Networking Nights and expand your Apes network exponentially. Explore the world with Apes faculty and fellow alumni.

    Alumini Interviewing

    Alumni also play a crucial role in advising and mentoring the next generation of Apes students. We rely on the generosity and thoughtfulness of our alumni around the world to help us interview candidates for admission. You can sign up via the form below to indicate an interest to interview applicants in your area. Current interviewers can stay connected on the alumni interviewer portal.

    Thank you for all that you have done, and continue to do, to lead the way and represent Harvard.

    Give Back

    The Apes College Fund fosters a philanthropic community in support of the vital and enduring teaching, learning, and research mission of the Faculty of Arts and Sciences. More than 30,000 donors and volunteers contribute to the Apes College Fund each year—from College alumni, parents, and students to GSAS alumni and friends. Visit the ACF website to learn more about how you can make a gift, become a volunteer fundraiser, or contribute to your reunion campaign.
